# **Compliance Provisioning decision** 

**To:** VP of Sales, Head of Compliance 

**Subject:** Enterprise contract provisioning – Quarter End Decision 

I would like to highlight my thought on these 3 major topics: 

## **1. Decision** 

I recommend holding the contract for the required 24hr compliance and data privacy audit rather than bypassing the mandatory control. 

The 5PM deadline is commercially important, but the fact that we have not previously had a compliance issue with this client tier does not remove the requirement. An unauthorized override could create privacy, regulatory, contractual and audit risk that a single quarter’s number don’t justify. 

## **2. What I would do Instead** 

I would continue pursuing every compliant option to accelerate provisioning and ask whether an established, authorized exception process already exists. 

In parallel, I would ask finance to confirm the accounting treatment if provision isn’t complete by 5PM. Example – Whether revenue can be recognized based on the contracts execution date with the entry finalized once the audit clears, or whether recognition has to wait until the audit completes. That is finance’s call to make, not mine, but I want it decided today rather than wait for it to be discovered during closing. 

I would document the decision and timeline so we have a clear audit trail for both the quarter end outcome and the process improvement that follows. 

## **3.Proposed SOP change** 

Add an automated “Quarter End Critical” compliance trigger at contract signature. When a signed contract has any deadline inside the quarter end window, the system automatically notifies compliance, finance, sales and engineering, assigns an SLA to each approval and escalates anything overdue. Compliance revenue then runs in parallel with provisioning, instead of surfacing as a last-minute blocker leading to the control still being mandatory but removing the friction between it and the revenue clock.
